About ProCheck

I'm Michael Austin. My full-time role is in marine sales with GA Checkpoint Motorsports & Marine in Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, on Canada's West Coast. I am also a qualified skipper. Over the years I've owned and operated everything from inflatables and sailboats to 30-foot powerboats, cruised most of the southern Gulf Islands, and crossed the Strait of Georgia (Salish Sea) to Vancouver Island more times than I can count. I previously owned and operated a marine fabrication business making canvas boat tops, and over the years I've worked with outdoor and marine equipment manufacturers in both the UK and Canada. I spend my working days on both sides of the deal — walking buyers around new and used boats, taking trades in, and answering the same questions you're likely asking as you read this.

ProCheck is that whole process, written down: buying new or used, from a dealer or a private seller — and then owning the boat well once it's yours. A private seller's photos never show a soft transom or milky lower-unit oil, and a shiny new boat still deserves hard questions about price, paperwork and delivery day. These checklists and logbooks are the ones I follow myself, and the ones I'd hand a friend: print them, take them to the boat, and buy — and boat — with your eyes open.

Away from the dealership, I'm still outdoors. You'll find me boating on the coast with my family, or camped beside an interior lake with a fishing rod out and the rooftop tent up. I'm also a UK-trained Mountain Leader, with winter and survival skills — the kind of background that teaches you a trip goes well when the planning does. ProCheck is that habit applied to boats: check the right things, in the right order, before they become problems.
